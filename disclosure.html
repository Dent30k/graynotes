Disclosure

An FTC-style note on advertising, affiliate programs, and what is (and is not) on these pages.

Updated August 2026 · Chicago

Graynotes is an editorial site. It may earn a commission from qualifying purchases if Amazon Associates or similar programs are attached later. No affiliate links, Amazon tracking IDs, or purchase buttons are live on these pages today. Product names are used to identify the objects under discussion. When a guide says “look on Amazon or the brand site,” that is plain language, not a checkout path.

If that changes, this page will be updated the same day, and any product link that can earn a commission will be labeled on the page that carries it. Until then, treat every SKU as a specimen, not a cart.

Independence

Manufacturers and retailers do not pay for placement in these guides. There are no sponsored slots in the list on the home page. Prices mentioned are historical or typical US street numbers, not an offer from Graynotes. A Black Friday print or a “seen around $40 extra” gap can invert; check current listings yourself.

What you will not find

  • Buy-now buttons, carts, or “shop this pick” modules
  • Amazon Associates tag parameters (tag=, assoc_tag, or similar)
  • ClickBank, supplement funnels, or outbound deal redirects

Questions about this policy belong on the about page. Privacy practices are on a separate note.
