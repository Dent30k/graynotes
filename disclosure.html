Disclosure

An FTC-style note on affiliate links on these pages.

Updated August 29, 2026 · Chicago

Graynotes is an editorial notebook, not a store. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Some notes carry a labeled Amazon link with tracking ID graynotes-20. Those links are disclosed on the page that carries them.

Product names identify the objects under discussion. A SKU without a labeled affiliate link is a specimen, not a checkout path. Check current listings yourself; street prices move.

Independence

Manufacturers and retailers do not pay for placement. There are no sponsored slots on the home page. Prices mentioned are historical or typical US street numbers, not an offer from Graynotes.

What you will not find

  • A cart, a countdown, or a fake shopper
  • ClickBank, supplement funnels, or outbound deal redirects
  • Unlabeled affiliate links

Questions belong on the about page. Privacy is a separate note.
