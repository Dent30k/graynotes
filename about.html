About Graynotes

A quiet place to sort objects that keep showing up in US kitchens, bedrooms, and under desks.

Updated August 2026 · Chicago

Graynotes is a small editorial site. It exists because product pages are noisy, comparison charts are paid, and a lot of “guides” are timed to a holiday weekend. This notebook is slower. Notes stay up after the sale ends. Dates are in America/Chicago. The first reader in mind is someone in the United States trying to decide whether an extra two quarts, an extra two inches of belt, or a firmer mattress is worth living with.

The voice is meant to sound like a margin note, not a launch. There are no countdown timers. There are no “as an Amazon Associate” lines, because there is no associate account attached to these pages. Product names appear because they are the subject, the way a field guide names a bird. If that ever changes — if a labeled product link appears — it will be obvious on the page. Until then, treat every SKU as a specimen, not a cart.

What gets a note

Kitchen form factors (dual drawer, stack, glass pod, toaster-oven hybrids). Walking pads and the desks they sit under. Mattresses, when the question is construction and firmness rather than a weekend coupon. A note has to answer a real fork: 8 qt or 10 qt, Luxury Firm or Plush, W50 or the sub-$150 pad that dies. Specs are taken from manufacturer sheets and from the packaging and listing mismatches people actually hit. Prices are snapshots, labeled as such, not live widgets.

What does not

Unboxing theater. Fake names in quotation marks. Medical claims. “I lost thirty pounds on this belt.” Anything that needs a pixel to prove it was read. Graynotes does not run analytics IDs, ad networks, or retargeting. The RSS feed is the intended subscription path.

Who writes this

Notes are written from Chicago. The kitchen and the desk in these pages are US 120V, US outlets, US return windows. UK model numbers show up only when a channel split matters (Ninja’s Dual Zone line is AF in the UK). If you are shopping a different voltage or a different warranty regime, the SKU table may still help; the price column will not.

Questions go to the contact page. Corrections are welcome. The point of a notebook is that it can be revised in ink.
