Privacy

A short account of what this site does not collect.

Updated August 2026 · Chicago

Graynotes is a static site. Pages are HTML and a stylesheet. There is no account system, no comment database, and no “personalization” layer. The site does not embed analytics scripts, advertising pixels, tag managers, or social trackers. There are no Google Analytics, Meta, TikTok, or similar IDs on these pages.

Logs

If these files are served from a host, that host may keep ordinary web-server logs (IP address, user agent, time, path). That is the host’s default behavior, not a Graynotes product. Those logs are not used here to build profiles or to retarget anyone. Graynotes does not run a separate analytics property on top of them.

Forms and email

The contact page includes a form that is not wired to a backend. If you send a note by email instead, you are using your own mail client. Email is retained only as long as it takes to answer, then deleted unless you ask to keep a thread open (a correction that needs a follow-up, for example).

Cookies and local storage

Graynotes does not set first-party cookies and does not write to local storage. If your browser shows a cookie from this host, it is likely from the hosting platform’s CDN or TLS layer, not from an editorial script.

Product links

Some notes include disclosed Amazon Associates links (tag graynotes-20). As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases. Those links are labeled on the page that carries them. Clicking them goes to Amazon. This site still does not run analytics pixels or ad networks of its own.

Feeds

The RSS file at feed.xml is a static document. Subscribing through a reader is between you and that reader. Graynotes cannot see who fetches the feed beyond whatever the host logs.

Questions about this note: use the contact page.
